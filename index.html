About Us

Studio Astra Architects is a Bangalore-based architecture and interior design studio founded by Ar. Prajwal Nagaraju and Ar. Manoj HJ. We specialize in architectural works, interior design, construction, and turnkey project execution. Our design philosophy combines functionality, craftsmanship, and modern aesthetics to create spaces that are elegant, practical, and timeless.
