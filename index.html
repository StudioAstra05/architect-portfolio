About Us

Studio Astra Architects is a premier, full-service architecture and interior design firm based in Bangalore. Founded by visionary architects Ar. Prajwal Nagaraju and Ar. Manoj HJ, our studio delivers bespoke design solutions that seamlessly bridge the gap between imagination and reality. We offer a comprehensive suite of services including architectural design, luxury interior design, construction management, and flawless turnkey project execution.

Our Philosophy

We believe that great architecture shapes the human experience. Our core design philosophy harmonizes pure functionality, meticulous craftsmanship, and contemporary aesthetics. We do not just build structures; we curate timeless, enduring environments.

Our Approach

  • Client-Centric Vision: Translating your unique lifestyle into physical spaces.
  • End-to-End Delivery: Managing everything from initial blueprints to final handover.
  • Uncompromising Quality: Partnering with skilled craftsmen using premium materials.
